Memory for Claude Code

Claude forgets.
Cogi doesn't.

Cogi the squirrel keeps every message you and Claude say, like nuts in its hollow. When something drops out of Claude's memory, Cogi brings it back.

Install in 2 minutes See how well it works

Free · runs on your computer · nothing leaves it

How it works

1

Collect

After every answer, Cogi picks up the new messages. You do nothing.

2

Keep

Each message is stored word for word, with its date, in your hollow: a folder on your computer.

3

Find

When Claude needs something from earlier, Cogi hands back the few messages that matter.

How Cogi does it →

Tested, not promised

As good as rereading the whole conversation

On the LoCoMo benchmark, answers from the 1,000 tokens Cogi hands back score 74.7%. Handing over the whole conversation, twenty times more text, scores 76.5%. On LongMemEval, the past session with the answer is among the first five Cogi finds for 99.0% of questions.

Why Cogi

Private

Your hollow is a folder on your computer. No cloud, no account.

Exact

You get the original words and when they were said. Cogi never makes things up.

Automatic

It saves after each answer and hands Claude what's relevant with each prompt.

Get started

One command in a terminal, with uv installed:

Terminal
claude plugin install cogito-mem --marketplace meridian21lab/cogito-mem
