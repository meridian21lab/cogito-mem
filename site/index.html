Memory for Claude Code

Claude forgets.
Cogi doesn't.

Cogi the squirrel keeps every message you and Claude say, like nuts in its hollow. When a long session compacts, or last month's idea matters again, Claude asks Cogi and gets your exact words back.

Install in 2 minutes See how well it works

Free · runs on your computer · nothing leaves it

How it works

1

Collect

After every answer, Cogi picks up the new messages. You do nothing.

2

Keep

Each message is stored word for word, with its date, in your hollow: a folder on your computer.

3

Find

When Claude needs something from earlier, Cogi hands back the few messages that matter.

Tested, not promised

The longer your history, the further ahead

A long project soon holds more than Claude can read at once. In LongMemEval's long test, each question hides its answer in about 480 past sessions, some 1.2 million tokens in all. Cogi puts the right session among its first five for 93% of the 500 questions, ahead of four other local memory plugins tested on the same questions.

Why Cogi

Private

Your hollow is a folder on your computer. No cloud, no account.

Exact

You get the original words and the date they were said, never an AI's summary.

Automatic

It saves after every answer, with nothing to do. Ask Claude “do you remember…?” and it searches all of it.

Get started

One command in a terminal, with uv installed:

Terminal
claude plugin install cogito-mem --marketplace meridian21lab/cogito-mem
