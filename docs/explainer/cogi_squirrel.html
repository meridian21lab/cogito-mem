For investors, partners and curious parents

The grown-up version

Problem

AI assistants forget between sessions, and each app keeps its own memory. Ideas mentioned in passing get lost, and people spend time explaining things again.

Product

cogito-mem: a memory that runs on your own computer. Assistants use two tools through MCP, append and query. Setup is one command, such as uvx cogito-mem install claude-code.

Why it's different
  • No AI model calls to store or search, so each message costs nothing to keep.
  • Exact words with dates and source positions, so every answer can be checked.
  • One memory shared by Claude Code, Codex and Claude Desktop.
  • Runs on a laptop CPU. The goal is an answer in under 100 milliseconds.
Who it's for

Developers and everyday users of AI chat assistants who want their past ideas back without having saved them.

Where it stands

October 2026: design agreed, first version being built. Next come short digests of long texts, built only from their own sentences. After that, pictures and screenshots.

For kids: one day Cogi will keep shiny pebbles too. Those are your pictures and screenshots.

NoteThe speed figure is a goal for the first version. It has not yet been measured on the build that will ship.

A true squirrel fact

Real squirrels bury far more nuts than they dig up again. The forgotten ones can sprout into new trees. With Cogi, your forgotten ideas get a chance to grow too.
