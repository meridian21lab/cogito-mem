# Marketing brief

Read this before picking a film's point. It says what Cogi is, who it is for, what we stand for and how we talk. The claims rules in `SKILL.md` ("What a film may claim") still decide what can be said out loud.

## What we are building

**Cogi is the long-term memory of your AI work, kept on your own computer.** It saves every message you and your AI assistant exchange, word for word, with its date, and finds the right one again in milliseconds, even in over a million tokens of history.

One line: **"Your AI forgets. Cogi keeps your exact words, dated, on your machine."**

Series tagline (once per film): **"Claude forgets. Cogi doesn't."**

## Where we stand next to Claude's own memory

We work alongside the assistant's memory; we don't replace or attack it.

| | The assistant's own memory | Cogi |
|---|---|---|
| Keeps | short notes about you: role, preferences, projects | the full record: every message, word for word, dated |
| Answers | "who is this person?" | "what exactly did we say, and when?" |
| Lives | in the vendor's cloud | in one folder on your computer |
| Covers | one vendor's apps | Claude Code and Codex in one memory, plus past chats imported from claude.ai and ChatGPT exports |

The film-friendly version: **"Your assistant remembers who you are. Cogi remembers what you said."**

## Our values (what every film should feel like)

1. **Your words, not a summary.** We hand back exactly what was said, with the date. Nothing is rewritten by an AI.
2. **Yours, at home.** One folder on your computer. No account, no API key, no cloud. Your memory never leaves your machine.
3. **Free and open.** MIT license, every line on GitHub, no bill and no AI calls to save a message.
4. **Honest and measured.** We publish what we measured, including where others do better. We never claim "never forgets" or savings we haven't measured.
5. **Light and quiet.** It works in the background, takes milliseconds, and stays out of the way.
6. **Friendly to the assistant.** Goldie is lovable. The joke is the goldfish memory, never the assistant's intelligence or its makers.

## Benefits

**What it does for you (functional)**
- Pick up a long project days or weeks later without retelling it.
- Nothing is lost when a long session compacts or a chat ends: the early messages are still there word for word.
- Find "what did we decide about X?" with the exact words and the date.
- Search across every project and both assistants at once.
- Free, no sign-up, about two minutes to install.

**How it feels (emotional)**
- Relief: you stop repeating yourself.
- Confidence: you can show the exact sentence and the date it was said.
- Control: your work history is a folder you own, not a vendor's file on you.
- Delight: a squirrel who is very proud of his hoard.

## Tone of voice

- Warm, curious, a little smug (Cogi), never snarky.
- Short sentences, plain words, present tense. One idea per line.
- Concrete over abstract: "the client hates blue" beats "user preferences".
- Humour from timing and understatement, as in a nature documentary.

## Never

- Mock Claude, Anthropic, OpenAI or any real company, or use their logos or mascots.
- Show features that don't exist yet (dashboards, timesheets, sharing cards, team sync) as if they were real.
- Say "nothing leaves your computer" (your prompts still go to your assistant). Say "your memory never leaves your machine".
- Use any number that isn't in the README, or round one up.
