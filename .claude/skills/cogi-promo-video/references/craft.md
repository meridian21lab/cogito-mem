# Craft rules, with numbers

Quality must not depend on taste in the moment. These rules come from the 2026-11-13 research:
- HyperFrames' motion, caption and sound references;
- Remotion's agent skills;
- Material motion timing;
- YouTube's loudness and Shorts overlays, measured on a live Short.

The helpers that implement them are in `promo/src/kit/`.

## Motion (`motion.ts`)

- **Nothing moves linearly except constant drift** (leaves, a slow pan).
  - Arrivals use a spring with overshoot: `springAt`, damping 10–14, stiffness 160–260.
  - Moves ease out (`EASE.out`, cubic-bezier 0.22, 1, 0.36, 1).
  - Exits ease in and take about two-thirds of the entry time.
- **Anticipation before action:** 3–5 frames the other way (a squash before a hop, a lean back before a throw).
- **Squash and stretch:** landings squash to 0.80–0.85; the airborne stretch is at most 1.15. `hop()` does both, plus a settle wobble.
- **Follow-through:** tails and ears lag the body by 2–4 frames and overshoot. Use a spring with low damping (6–8) for a tail flick.
- **Stagger** siblings (acorns, sparkles, words) 2–4 frames apart, never all at once.
- **Hold** a pose at least 6 frames so it reads; a comic reaction at least 0.6 s.
- **Idle life is always on:** breathing, blinks every 2.5–4.5 s, ear flicks, a lazy tail (`idleCogi`, `idleGoldie`). A character never freezes.

## Timing

- **Every beat lands on the start frame of the word that causes it** (`tl.word`). A sound effect plays one frame after the picture that causes it.
- **Comic timing lives in the gaps between lines:**
  - 0.6–1.2 s after a punchline, for the reaction;
  - 0.15–0.3 s between lines that run together.
- **Keep the picture moving:** no stretch over 3 s without visible change (`npm run check` fails on it). In the first 10 s, change the shot or the action every 2–4 s.
- **Length:** 35–50 s; about 80–110 words of narration.

## Camera (`Camera.tsx`)

- **A shot is the world point at the frame's centre plus a zoom.** Zoom moves interpolate in log space, so push-ins feel even.
- **Push in 10–30% over a line** for emphasis. A **snap zoom** (×1.4–1.5 in 4–6 frames) makes a comic close-up.
- **Whip pans** take 10–12 frames with `EASE.inOut`. The directional blur comes from the camera's own sideways speed (the first 12 px per frame stay sharp, capped at 36 px). A zoom alone stays sharp.
- **Hand-held drift** is always on (about ±5 px, ±0.35°). Shakes run 8–14 px and decay over 14 frames, on impacts only.
- **Parallax:** sky 0.05, far trees 0.3, mid trees 0.6, set 1.0, foreground leaves 1.3.
- **Both cuts:**
  - keep the key action inside the 4:5 band (world y 285–1635 at zoom 1);
  - in close-ups, keep anything important lower than about 650 world px above the shot's centre;
  - check `sheet-feed.jpg`.

## Light and finish (`Finish.tsx`)

- **Key light** is warm, from the top left: the sun glow in the sky layer, highlights on the left of round shapes.
- **Contact shadows** under everything that touches a surface: ink at opacity 0.12–0.15, flattened ellipses. Shadows shrink as a character lifts.
- **Vignette** is about 30% ink at the corners; **grain** is fractal noise at 9% overlay, re-seeded every frame.
- **Light leaks** go only on reveals and the tagline: at most 2 per film, about 40 frames each.

## Transitions

- Stay inside the world when you can: whip pans, push-ins and pull-backs. No cross-dissolves.
- Cogi's tail wipe goes into the title card.
- Use at most 3 kinds of transition per film.

## Type

- **Captions** (`Captions.tsx`):
  - Outfit Bold 84 px, cream with an 11 px ink stroke and a hard drop shadow;
  - at most 3 words per group, broken at punctuation;
  - the spoken word lights orange and scales 1.07;
  - placed at y 1450 (9:16) and y 1165 (4:5), 110 px side margins;
  - hidden on lines whose words are already on screen (tagline, end card).
- **On-screen text** is at least 40 px at the shot's zoom. Species cards are 52 and 38 px.
- **Fonts:** Outfit Bold for display, Work Sans for body (both SIL OFL, in `public/fonts`).

## Sound (`Sound.tsx`, `scripts/render.sh`)

- **Levels are set by measured loudness,** never by ear:
  - narration about −18 LUFS, keeping 40% of its natural range, so a whisper stays a whisper;
  - sound effects about −21 LUFS, trimmed down 2–4 dB when repeated;
  - the music bed about −22 LUFS in the gaps, ducked 10 dB under the voice (5 frames of attack, 14 of release), fading in over 8 frames and out over the last 40.
- **Master:** −14 LUFS integrated, true peak ≤ −1 dBTP. Two-pass ffmpeg `loudnorm` (target −2 dBTP: AAC encoding adds up to about 0.7 dB of overshoot), AAC 192k at 48 kHz.
- **Sound effects:** each one has a cause on screen. Rotate 2–3 takes of anything used more than once. Give clips one-frame (30 ms) edge fades.
- **Music:** generated by ElevenLabs Music to the film's length. Instrumental, playful orchestral (pizzicato, marimba, woodwinds), light bass because phone speakers lose it, and a resolving final phrase.

## The hook (first 2 seconds)

- Frame 1 shows a character in motion. Never a title card, never black.
- Name the problem or the character within 2 s (the binocular open on Goldie, "Behold! The AI assistant.").
- The first comic beat lands by 8 s.
